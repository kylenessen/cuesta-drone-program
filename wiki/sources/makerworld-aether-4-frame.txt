DESCRIPTION (designer's text, lightly condensed)

This is the Aether 4, a fully 3D-printed unibody FPV frame for 4/4.5 inch drones,
inspired by nature and biomimetic design.

What is it good at:
- No frame assembly required. Unibody design, printed in one piece.
- Rigid yet lightweight, strong but still under 40 grams.
- True X layout, good pitch authority. Not a DC nor squashed X.
- No prop in view (DJI O3 wide-angle mode).
- Low-cost. One roll of filament prints up to 20 copies.
- Aerodynamic design. Efficient and low cruising throttle.
- Sub 250g with 10min+ air time.
- Modification and accessories available. Customization: choose your own print
  settings, materials, or modify the model.

Data you may want to know:
- FC and ESC: 20 x 20mm stack OR 25.5 x 25.5mm AIO
- Motors: 9 x 9mm pitch OR 12 x 12mm pitch
- Prop: maximum 4.5 inch
- VTX: any VTX the Pavo 20 chassis supports (DJI O3, Caddx Vista, RunCam Link)
- Motor pitch: ~185mm x 185mm, X layout
- Sweep angle: 28 deg
- Frame weight: ~40g @ 2 walls

Setup examples:

Sub250 Setup (designer's setup): TOW ~250g. Motor Darwin 1504 kv2300 or HLG 1404
kv2800. Prop GemFan 4525-3 (4.5 inch) or GemFan 4024-2 (4 inch). Battery Boshi LiPO
P7 850mAh 4S, ~93g. Cruising time ~12 min. Freestyle time ~6 min.

FreeStyle Setup: frame printed with 3 walls. Motor 2006 kv1900 or 2204 kv1800. Prop
GemFan 4525-3 (4.5 inch). Battery 6S 850mAh-1300mAh.

Long Range Setup: motor 1404 KV2800. Prop GemFan 4525-3 (4.5 inch) or 4 inch low
pitch 2-blade props. Battery 4S 3000mAh 18650 Li-ion. Cruising time >30min.

HOW TO PRINT (designer's guidance)

The O3 unit and VTX sit inside the BetaFPV Pavo 20 VTX chassis which is NOT provided
in the print file; purchase one to complete a video-equipped drone.

- The frame is designed to print without infill; infill adds little to no strength.
- The frame is optimized for the placement and rotation in the print file.
- The supports in the print file are painted and necessary for better edges and
  surface finish.

Other suggestions:
- Printing with PLA is just fine.
- Rigid filaments are preferred over flexible ones. Carbon-reinforced filament
  offers even greater advantages.
- For extreme uses such as heavy freestyle or racing, a high-temperature filament is
  essential to prevent the motor becoming loose due to heat building up under the
  motor.
- This frame is sensitive to vibrations. Ensure motors and props are well balanced
  dynamically.

FLIGHT CONTROLLER SETUP (Betaflight 4.5+)

The frame works well on default Betaflight tuning. If vibrations or jello appear,
check motor and prop balance first. For low-frequency wobble or warm motors, the
designer recommends starting with lower D gains and heavier gyro and D-term filters
(specific CLI values published on the page).

Make sure the motor screws are tightened before flight; plastic can bounce screws
out after a flight. Or use screw locker (glue). Avoid high load which heats up the
plastic under the motor; it will cause loose screws and vibrations. This is the key
to a clean flight. The designer shows a vibration analysis where loose motor screws
after a heavy-load flight made the next flight noisier until retightened.

DESIGN NOTES (designer's story, condensed)

The designer wanted a 4-inch sub-250g drone and found only similar-looking carbon
fiber frames. 3D printing allows biomimetic one-piece structures without molds.
The frame is lighter than comparable carbon frames because carbon builds need
screws, CNC parts, and pillars to form 3D structure. The design lowers the
propellers and raises the camera so props stay out of view while keeping a regular
X layout. Adjustments were made to increase rigidity and match the resonance
frequency of carbon frames.

RELEVANT BUILD COMMENTS

@Wheezy (2024-10): Build with JHEMCU GHF405 40A AIO, RCinpower 1404 2750kv motors,
Caddx Vista, SpeedyBee ELRS nano, Gemfan 4023 tri-blade props, CNHL 4S 850mAh.
Ditched metal PLA for a stronger PLA+ in bright orange. "It flies pretty well for
what it is. It's definitely not a frame you can bang around and crash into things,
it handles little knocks and stuff fine but will break in a decent crash." Suggests
slightly larger motors (1604, 1806, or 2004) or staying low KV on 1404s "because
they really aren't rated for 4 inch props, they work, but they can get hot if you
start pushing them too much." Battery note: 850mAh Gaoneng packs took about 20g off
versus the CNHL listed.

@Keresztes (2024-12): Printed with 0.4mm nozzle in Bambu PETG-CF, no infill, 3
walls. Built with BetaFPV AIO 25A FC, BetaFPV 1404 4500KV motors, Diatone Mamba M22
GPS, Rush Tank Mini VTX, Foxeer Cat 2 camera, TBS Crossfire. "Flies great with stock
Betaflight PIDs. I was worried the frame wouldn't be stiff enough but was pleasantly
surprised." Weight without battery: 143.2g.
