# NSSL UAS Standard Operating Procedure

**NSSL UAS Standard Operating Procedure** is the published standard operating procedure of the NOAA National Severe Storms Laboratory's UAS program, revised July 19, 2023. It is a rare example of a complete, publicly posted institutional drone SOP, and it is useful as a model for how organizations formalize field crew practices: a two-person minimum crew with an explicit prohibition on deploying alone, required safety vests with mission-dependent boots, hard hats, and eye protection, crew safety equipment including two-way radios, first aid kit, and fire extinguisher, a 16 hour crew duty day limit, weather recorded on the preflight checklist and reevaluated before takeoff, and lost-link and emergency procedures.
