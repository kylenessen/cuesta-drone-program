# BM Aether 4 MakerWorld Listing

**BM Aether 4 - The 4 inch unibody FPV drone frame** is the MakerWorld model page for the 3D-printed airframe under consideration for the AERO 100 student build, published by the designer "Dr. J. Ma" in July 2024. It is the primary evidence for the frame's specifications (unibody print, about 40 grams, 20x20mm stack mounting, 1404 to 2004 motors on 9mm or 12mm bolt patterns, 4.5 inch maximum props), the designer's setup examples from sub-250-gram cruiser to long range, the print guidance (no infill, rigid or carbon-reinforced filament, vibration sensitivity, motor screws loosening in warm plastic), the accessory ecosystem including a GPS holder, and the MakerWorld-exclusive license adopted in October 2024. Builder comments preserved with the extraction document real-world evidence: a GPS-equipped build flying on stock tune, and motor heat on 1404 motors pushed with 4 inch props.

## Original Source

The extracted page text is preserved in this folder as `makerworld-aether-4-frame.txt`, captured July 15, 2026 from https://makerworld.com/en/models/541413-bm-aether-4-the-4-inch-unibody-fpv-drone-frame. MakerWorld blocks simple fetches, so the capture came through a browser session.

## Extraction Quality

The extraction keeps the designer's full description, specification data, setup examples, print and tuning guidance, license terms, and design notes, lightly condensed. Site navigation, print-profile interface, purchase links, and unrelated comments were stripped. Two builder comment threads with direct evidentiary value (a PETG-CF GPS build and a motor-heat report) are preserved near-verbatim. The designer's specific Betaflight CLI filter values were summarized rather than copied; consult the live page before tuning.
