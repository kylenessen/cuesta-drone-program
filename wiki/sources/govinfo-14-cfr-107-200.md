# 14 CFR 107.200

**14 CFR 107.200** is the Part 107 waiver policy and requirements section. It states the FAA standard for issuing a certificate of waiver, the required contents of a waiver request, and the FAA's authority to prescribe additional limitations or conditions on the waiver.
