# ICAO Safety Management Manual, Doc 9859

Doc 9859, the *Safety Management Manual*, is ICAO's implementation guidance for safety management. The [official store record](https://store.icao.int/en/safety-management-manual-doc-9859) and [ICAO guidance page](https://www.icao.int/safety-management/guidance-material) identify the fourth edition, 2018, as of September 22, 2026. Announcements about developing a fifth edition should not be treated as proof of its release.

## Access and conversion status

ICAO's [access page](https://www.icao.int/safety-management/access-icao-annexes-and-guidance) offers free read-only eLibrary access and purchase routes. The [Safety Management Implementation site](https://www.icao.int/safety-management/SMI/SMM) provides chapter navigation. An [ICAO-hosted fourth-edition PDF](https://www.icao.int/APAC/Meetings/2019%20COSCAPSEA%20iSTARS/9859_cons_en.pdf) was located in regional meeting materials. Its public availability does not make the publication public domain.

Full text was not copied or converted. These are concise source notes, not a substitute for the manual. A user-provided copy is needed for full-text conversion. No purchase or restricted-access workaround was attempted.

## Suggested reading

The SMI chapter guide identifies Chapter 2 on fundamentals, Chapter 3 on safety culture, Chapter 4 on safety performance management, and Chapter 9 on operator SMS. These are the most relevant starting points for a drone instructor. Chapter 8 concerns State safety management. In that context, State means a country, not the State of California. See the [curriculum proposal](../analysis/sms-for-drone-curriculum.md) for the locally authored application.
