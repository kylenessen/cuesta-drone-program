# AC 107-2A

**AC 107-2A**, Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS), is the FAA Advisory Circular that explains how to comply with 14 CFR Part 107. Dated February 1, 2021, it incorporates the Operations Over People and Remote Identification rules and covers registration, remote pilot certification, operating rules, night and civil twilight operations, waivers, risk management, and preflight and maintenance practice across 104 pages. Where the regulation states a requirement in one sentence, this circular explains what the FAA considers acceptable compliance.

For course development it is the best single narrative source: it is authoritative, current on the 2021 rule changes, and written to be read rather than parsed.
