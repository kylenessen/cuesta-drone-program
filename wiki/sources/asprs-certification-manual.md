# ASPRS Certification Manual

The **ASPRS Certification Manual**, formally the ASPRS Certification and Recertification Guidelines, is the governing document for the certification program of the American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing. The revision preserved here is the version ASPRS publishes as current. It covers the Scientist and Technologist certification levels and their five specialty areas, the application requirements and references, the fee table, Prometric examination administration, the retake policy, recertification, and the examination content matrices, including the Certified UAS Technologist matrix.

Notably, this current revision contains no section on the Geospatial Intern Program and no intern rows in its fee table, both of which appeared in the 2019 revision preserved as [ASPRS Certification Manual (2019)](/sources/asprs-certification-manual-2019.md). That absence is itself evidence and drives several open questions on the intern pathway page.

## Original Source

The original PDF is preserved in this folder as `asprs-certification-manual.pdf`, downloaded 2026-07-10 from ASPRS at https://www.asprs.org/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/ASPRS_Certification_Manual.pdf. The document carries no revision date on its cover.
