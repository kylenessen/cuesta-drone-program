# ICAO Annex 19

Annex 19, *Safety Management*, is ICAO's international safety management standards and recommended practices. ICAO means the International Civil Aviation Organization. It is the issuing organization, not a separate document in the requested reading list.

## Edition and access record

Checked September 22, 2026. ICAO's [2026 publications catalog](https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/publications/catalogue/cat_2026_en.pdf) identifies the third edition, November 2025, incorporating Amendments 1 and 2. It supersedes previous editions on November 26, 2026. ICAO's [SARPs page](https://www.icao.int/safety-management/standards-and-recommended-practices-sarps) distinguishes adoption on June 23, 2025, effectiveness on November 4, 2025, and applicability on November 26, 2026. The second edition remains the applicable baseline at the research date.

The [official access page](https://www.icao.int/safety-management/access-icao-annexes-and-guidance) links to free read-only eLibrary access and purchase options. The [store category](https://store.icao.int/en/shop-by-areas/safety/safety-management) identifies the third edition. Full publication text was not copied or converted. This copyrighted publication needs a user-provided copy for full-text conversion. These are bibliographic and access notes based on public ICAO pages, not an extraction of the Annex.

## Relevance

The [FAA circular](ac-120-92d.md), Appendix A, supplies the accessible Part 5 to ICAO SMS crosswalk. For the forthcoming change, ICAO's SARPs page identifies expansion to certified RPAS operators authorized for international operations and maintenance organizations serving them. This does not establish a new blanket SMS requirement for domestic Part 107 teaching flights. See [Part 5 and the international framework](../regulations/part-5-safety-management-systems.md).
