# NCAT TOP Level 2 Course Syllabus

The **NCAT TOP Level 2 Course Syllabus** is a community college syllabus for a three credit hour course delivering AUVSI Trusted Operator (TOP) Level 2 certification, published by the National Center for Autonomous Technology (NCAT) as a template for its partner schools. It was written by Jaykob Miller, adjunct instructor at Northland Community & Technical College, dated April 24, 2025, and is the clearest public evidence of what teaching TOP Level 2 for credit actually looks like. The course outline names six content modules (Operational Overview, Navigation, Meteorology and Environmental Elements, Human Factors, Personal Flight Assessment, and Quality Assurance) plus a review week and finals, graded through six end of lesson knowledge checks (60 percent), a final exam (15 percent), and a final exercise described as a "check-ride style application of concepts" (25 percent). The course objectives extend beyond piloting into UAS architecture, data links and frequency propagation, payloads including electro optical, infrared, and synthetic aperture radar sensors, OSHA safety standards, and FAA documentation requirements. The listed text is Barnhart's Introduction to Unmanned Aircraft Systems.
