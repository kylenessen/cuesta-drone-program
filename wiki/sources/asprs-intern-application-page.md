# ASPRS Intern Application Page

The **ASPRS Intern Application Page**, titled "How To Apply - Interns" on the former ASPRS website, is the most detailed public statement of the Geospatial Intern Program mechanics. It states the eligibility rule (an active student preparing to graduate in a spatial sciences degree program at an approved institution of higher education), the application package (application, fee, one reference from an academic advisor or relevant faculty member, an official transcript validating a minimum of two courses in the spatial sciences, and a Code of Ethics declaration), the six month examination window after peer review notification, the intern designations awarded on passing, the conversion windows (ten years to complete the six year experience requirement for Scientist interns, five years to complete the three year requirement for Technologist interns, with extensions only for active duty military service), and the conversion procedure (written documentation plus three references to ASPRS Headquarters).

Its intern designation list names only the six pre-2017 categories (Photogrammetrist, Remote Sensing Mapping Scientist, GIS/LIS Mapping Scientist, and the three corresponding Technologist Intern titles), so it predates the UAS and Lidar certification categories. Whether the intern pathway now extends to Certified UAS Technologist is an open question on the pathway page.
