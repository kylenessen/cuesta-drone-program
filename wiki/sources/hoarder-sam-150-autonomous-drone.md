# Hoarder Sam $150 Autonomous Drone Video

**"I Built a $150 Autonomous Drone (Step by Step)"** is a YouTube build video published by the channel Hoarder Sam in October 2025, documenting a complete GPS-capable quadcopter built from AliExpress parts for a claimed $150. The aircraft is an F450-frame quad running iNav 8 on a SpeedyBee F405 Wing Mini, an RC airplane flight controller chosen because its servo-style headers accept $5 airplane speed controllers without custom wiring. It flies over 20 minutes on a 3S 5000mAh RC-car battery, carries up to two pounds of payload on a servo-operated drop mechanism, holds position on GPS, and can run autonomous waypoint missions. It carries no camera and flies line of sight. The video is the primary evidence for the build's parts list and prices, its assembly and configuration sequence, and the deliberate design philosophy: big props on small cheap motors for efficiency, dumb PWM electronics that expose how the aircraft works, and a payload-platform framing ("a drone with a job") rather than FPV freestyle.
