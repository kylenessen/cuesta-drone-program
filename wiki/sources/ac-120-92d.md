# AC 120-92D

AC 120-92D, *Safety Management Systems for Aviation Service Providers*, was issued by the FAA on May 21, 2024. The [FAA catalog record](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/1042733) listed it as active when checked September 22, 2026. This advisory circular explains acceptable ways to implement Part 5 and also supports voluntary SMS development. It is guidance, not an independent regulation.

## Reading priorities

Read Chapter 2 for the relationship between safety risk management and safety assurance. Use Chapter 3 alongside the regulatory sections. Appendix G explains small operator implementation. Appendix B offers sample risk worksheets, Appendix C covers safety policy and a code of ethics, and Appendix A maps Part 5 to the ICAO SMS framework available when the circular was issued. That 2024 crosswalk is not evidence of the later Annex 19 Amendment 2 changes.
