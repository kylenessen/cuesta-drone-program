Syllabus for UAST xxxx – Trusted Operator (TOP) Level 2 (3 Credit Hours)
1.  COURSE DESCRIPTION:  This course offers students training and academics leading to Trusted Operator (TOP) Level 2 certification.  The goal of this course is to provide students enhanced coursework leading to the TOP Level 2 professional credential.
2. RATIONALE: The UAS career field is ever-growing, spanning a multitude of industries in private practice, education, government, etc. With an increase in UAS job market opportunities comes the necessity for UAS operators to distinguish themselves as specialists in order to secure the jobs they want. The Trusted Operator (TOP) professional certification exists to help UAS operators stand out among their peers and give them a leading edge in the UAS workforce.
3. COURSE OBJECTIVES: By the end of this course students should be able to:
Explain aviation management principles and FAA documentation requirements for uncrewed systems.
Identify the components of UAS.
Explain the effects of weather on UAS flight operations.
Define OSHA safety standards and safety principles utilized in the aircraft maintenance environment.
Explain basic aerodynamic principles and aircraft performance characteristics.
Describe concepts and materials used in UAS construction.
Understand the systems of Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (UAS) architecture to include local area networks, network transmission, data link functions, attributes, and frequency propagation.
Identify UAS payloads including sensing and imaging sensors, electro optical/infrared, design, environmental conditions, and synthetic aperture radar.
