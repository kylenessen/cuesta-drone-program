# 14 CFR Part 5 source

Title 14, Part 5 of the Code of Federal Regulations establishes FAA Safety Management System requirements. This source captures the current eCFR text for the September 22, 2026 safety curriculum research. The page displayed Title 14 as up to date through September 21, 2026. That is a title-wide currency date, not a claim that Part 5 changed that day.

## Original source and extraction

The [official eCFR page](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-5) is the live reference. The [plain-text snapshot](14-cfr-part-5-2026-09-22.txt) preserves all 25 numbered sections, §§ 5.1 through 5.97, across Subparts A through F. The web retrieval tool supplied the page body because direct HTML and API downloads were blocked. Navigation, heading markup, and inline link markup were removed. Authority text was joined across a broken retrieval line. Paragraph labels and amendment references remain. This is an extraction of the regulation, not an authored summary or an official published edition.

The January 1, 2025 annual edition was also consulted through [GovInfo's Part 5 PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title14-vol1/pdf/CFR-2025-title14-vol1-part5.pdf). It was used as a cross-check, not substituted for the current eCFR capture. Use the live eCFR for future currency checks.

## Reading priorities

Start with § 5.1 for applicability and §§ 5.3 and 5.5 for definitions and the four SMS components. Subparts B through F supply the teaching structure. The [Part 5 article](../regulations/part-5-safety-management-systems.md) explains the distinction between binding requirements and proposed classroom adaptations.
