# How Pacific Gas and Electric Company Uses Unmanned Aircraft

**How Pacific Gas and Electric Company Uses Unmanned Aircraft to Augment Services in California** is a Vertical Mag feature by Elan Head, published November 12, 2025, reporting from inside PG&E's Aviation Services department. It is the best public window into how a mature utility drone program actually runs day to day: more than 300,000 pole and tower inspections by UAS in 2024, around 80 UAS pilots in the field daily, the Flight Core communications center that delivers weather briefings and flight clearances to every PG&E aircraft, Garmin inReach satellite communicators issued to remote pilots, the knock-and-introduce-yourself customer contact procedure, Flight Core's tracking of hostile customer locations with corporate security involvement, dock-based BVLOS remote operations from Concord, and drone rope-pulling for line stringing. Named sources include director of Aviation Services Chris Courtney, UAS and fixed-wing operations manager Kellen Kirk, supervisor Daniel Toothman, and remote pilot Daniel Lira.
