# Flying Near Energized Infrastructure

**Flying Near Energized Infrastructure** will cover the hazards and working practices of drone operations around energized power lines, substations, and related utility equipment, including minimum approach distances, electromagnetic interference with aircraft electronics, compass disturbance near conductors, and the coordination asset owners expect. This is a draft stub held for a dedicated research pass (GitHub issue 22). Until it is written, the cited material on safety near energized equipment lives in [Infrastructure Inspection Operations](/topics/infrastructure-inspection-operations.md), and the radio-link side of interference is covered in [Drone Radio Links and Interference](/topics/drone-radio-links-and-interference.md).
