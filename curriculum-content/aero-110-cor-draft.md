### Proposal Rationale

Drone mapping and remote sensing represent one of the fastest-growing segments of commercial UAS employment. Industries including surveying, construction, agriculture, environmental monitoring, and civil engineering increasingly rely on drone-collected aerial data processed into orthomosaics, elevation models, volumetric calculations, and vegetation index maps. This course is the advanced applications course in the AERO sequence, building directly on the flight proficiency and Part 107 airmanship taught in AERO 100. It prepares students for professional mapping positions requiring remote sensing fundamentals, RTK positioning, ground control methodology, photogrammetric processing, accuracy validation to ASPRS standards, and multispectral data collection and interpretation for agricultural applications. Course content aligns with the body of knowledge tested by the ASPRS Certified UAS Technologist credential. San Luis Obispo County's active construction, agriculture, and environmental sectors provide direct employment pathways for graduates with these skills.

### Catalog Description

Prepares students to plan and execute professional drone mapping and remote sensing operations, from sensor fundamentals through deliverable production. Covers remote sensing and camera fundamentals, RTK positioning and ground control point methodology for survey-grade accuracy, photogrammetric processing using industry-standard software, and accuracy validation against ASPRS standards. Includes multispectral imaging, vegetation indices, and agricultural applications including prescription map production. Students produce orthomosaics, digital elevation models, volumetric calculations, and vegetation index maps meeting professional specifications. Emphasizes practical skills applicable to surveying, construction, agriculture, environmental monitoring, and civil engineering contexts.

## Topics and Scope

### Detailed Topical Outline

1. **Topic:** Remote Sensing Foundations
   - Remote sensing platforms and the resolution versus coverage tradeoff
   - Electromagnetic spectrum: visible, infrared, and ultraviolet regions
   - Digital imaging: sensors, color, RAW versus JPEG, bit depth
   - Drone payloads: passive versus active sensors, RGB to LiDAR
   - **Objectives:** (Obj 1)

2. **Topic:** Cameras and Image Quality
   - Mechanical versus electronic shutter, sensor size, focal length
   - Exposure: shutter, aperture, ISO, and the motion blur floor
   - Ground sample distance: formula, target requirements, altitude tradeoffs
   - Lighting and sun angle: shadows, overcast, hotspot effect, sun glint
   - **Objectives:** (Obj 1, 4)

3. **Topic:** Positioning and Geodesy
   - GNSS fundamentals and standalone accuracy limits
   - RTK and PPK positioning: base and rover, fix versus float, NTRIP and CRTN
   - Map projections and coordinate systems: UTM, State Plane, EPSG codes
   - Vertical datums and geoid models: ellipsoidal versus orthometric heights
   - Ground control points: control versus checkpoints, network design, survey procedure
   - Mapping accuracy: RMSE reporting, ASPRS standards, checkpoint validation
   - **Objectives:** (Obj 2, 3)

4. **Topic:** Mission Planning and Field Operations
   - Overlap, altitude, speed, capture rate, and terrain following
   - Mission planning software: DJI Pilot 2, UgCS, and the planner landscape
   - Field operations best practices: checklists, go/no-go judgment, in-field image checks
   - Field power management: batteries, charging, generators, power stations
   - **Objectives:** (Obj 4)

5. **Topic:** Photogrammetry and Products
   - Structure from motion: feature matching, bundle adjustment, georeferencing
   - Orthomosaics: orthorectification, artifacts, true ortho, absolute position
   - Elevation models: DSM versus DTM, contours, volumetrics, formats
   - Photogrammetry software: Metashape, Pix4D, DroneDeploy, OpenDroneMap
   - Geospatial data management: project structure, archiving, flight logs, delivery
   - LiDAR concepts: where active laser mapping takes over from photogrammetry
   - **Objectives:** (Obj 5)

6. **Topic:** Agricultural Remote Sensing
   - Multispectral imaging: NDVI, the red edge, measuring greenness
   - Vegetation indices: NDRE, soil-adjusted indices, limits of interpretation
   - Multispectral field practice: calibration panels, band registration, multispectral versus RGB
   - Agricultural applications: scouting, stand counts, irrigation and water stress, nutrient management
   - Prescription maps: management zones and machine-readable output files
   - **Objectives:** (Obj 6)

7. **Topic:** The Mapping Profession
   - Applications of drone mapping: products clients buy and decisions they feed
   - Change detection and repeat mapping
   - Limits of drone mapping: where photogrammetry stops being the right tool
   - Mission cost analysis: mobilization, estimating, insurance, pricing
   - Drone mapping and licensed surveying: the California PLS Act boundary
   - NDAA compliance and Blue UAS: federal restrictions and the platform landscape
   - **Objectives:** (Obj 7)

   **Builds to (AERO 110 Objective 4):** Mapping mission design balances coverage area, altitude, speed, and capture rate against aircraft endurance and weather windows; students apply AERO 100 performance calculations to size missions and battery plans for full mapping days.

4. **Prerequisite skill (AERO 100 Objective):** Perform preflight inspection to identify maintenance deficiencies that adversely affect aircraft performance.
   **Builds to (AERO 110 Objective 4):** Field operations best practices extend AERO 100 preflight inspection discipline into multi-battery, multi-flight mapping days where equipment readiness determines mission success.
