# AERO 100: Basic UAV Operator - Content Ideas

This document captures content ideas for AERO 100 as they come up. It serves as a running list to draw from during curriculum development.

## Battery Safety and Maintenance (Could Be a Whole Lecture)

This is crucial content that deserves significant time.

### Hazards of LiPo Batteries
- Show videos of LiPo fires to emphasize the danger
- Batteries are very dangerous and students need to understand that

### Charging Best Practices
- Always charge on tile or non-flammable surface
- Away from flammables
- Have the correct type of fire extinguisher on hand
- Looking for signs of damage before charging

### LiPo Charging Characteristics
- The charging rate is not uniform
- Going from 30% to 80% is much faster than going from 80% to 100%
- Going from 90% to 100% is even slower
- This knowledge is genuinely helpful in the field when trying to get back in the air

### Thermal Management
- Equipment heats up fast in direct sun
- Can cause equipment to seize up
- Batteries do not like to be charged when very hot

## Remote ID

Probably worth mentioning, maybe as a "back in my day" topic. Most new DJI drones have it integrated, but students may encounter older aircraft that do not have it by default.

## Flight Exercises

### Precision Maneuvering
- Who can draw the most perfect circle
- Flying to targets on command

### Dynamic Exercises (Idea to Explore)
- Programmable lights course where students do not know which one will turn on next
- Have to fly to it on a dime
- Budget approximately $500 for materials to build training course

## Topics from Approved Course Outline

The following are from the official AERO 100 course outline and must be covered:

### Maintenance and Preflight Inspection Procedures
- Regulatory requirements for maintenance
- Preflight inspection
- Postflight inspection
- Manufacturer inspection intervals
- Battery maintenance
- Flight and maintenance logs
- Maintenance, repair, and replacement of components

### UAV Flight Operations
- Takeoff
- Aircraft control
- Preflight programming
- Turns around a point
- Aircraft positioning
- Altitude control
- Sensor zoom/pan/tilt
- Airspeed control
- Approach
- Landing

### Emergency Procedures
- Contingency planning
- Ground emergencies
- Inflight emergencies
- Emergency communications

### Human Factors
- Health and environmental factors
- Decision making and judgment
- Risk management and aeronautical decision making

### Aircraft Performance
- Weight and balance calculations
- Density altitude
- Takeoff and climb performance
- Endurance
- Landing distance
- Aerodynamics
- Ground effect

### Aviation Weather Theory, Sources, and Effects on Performance
- Fronts, stability, clouds, and thunderstorms
- Fog
- Structural icing and frost
- Weather sources for aviation operations
- The AWC Home Page (NWS Aviation Weather Center)
- Practical sources for the remote pilot
- Automated weather reporting stations

### Airport and Off-Airport Operations
- Operations at non-tower airports
- Operations at airports with control towers
- Off-airport operations
- Flying at night

### Airspace and Navigation
- Aeronautical charts
- Knowing where you can fly
- Geographical coordinates
- Chart reading
- Airspace
- Correcting for wind drift
- Special use airspace

### Regulations, Procedures, and Other Aeronautical References
- UAS terms and abbreviations
- Types of UAV, components and uses of UAV
- Government regulations and other procedures publications
- FAA chart supplements
- Advisory circulars
- Notices to airmen
- Aviation media

## Student Learning Outcomes (From Course Outline)

1. Demonstrate mastery of FAA Part 107 small unmanned aerial vehicle knowledge topics, including airspace, aviation weather, aircraft loading, emergency procedures, crew resource management, aeronautical decision-making, and airport operations, by passing a written exam on these topics with a minimum passing score of 80 percent.

2. Safely maneuver a small unmanned aerial vehicle through an open test lane using the National Institute of Standards and Technology Level 2 standard course within the NIST-prescribed time limit (25 minutes).
