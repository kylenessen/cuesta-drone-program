# AERO 100: Basic UAV Operator - Content Ideas

This document captures content ideas for AERO 100 as they emerge. It serves as a running list to draw from during curriculum development.

## Battery Safety and Maintenance

This topic warrants significant lecture time, potentially a full session.

### LiPo Hazards

Students need to understand that batteries are genuinely dangerous. Videos of LiPo fires effectively convey the risks and tend to make an impression.

### Charging Best Practices

Cover proper charging procedures: non-flammable surfaces (tile is ideal), distance from flammable materials, appropriate fire extinguisher types, and pre-charge inspection for damage.

### Charging Characteristics

LiPo charging rates are non-linear. Charging from 30% to 80% is significantly faster than 80% to 100%, with the final 10% being slowest. This has practical field implications when students need to maximize available flight time.

### Thermal Management

Equipment overheats quickly in direct sun and can fail as a result. Batteries should not be charged while hot. Students working outdoors need to plan for thermal considerations.

## Remote ID

Worth covering as context rather than core content. Most current DJI drones include Remote ID by default, but students may encounter older aircraft without the capability. This could work as a brief historical perspective on how the regulatory landscape has evolved.

## Introduction to Operational Applications

Per Ron's notes, AERO 100 will introduce operational applications that the advanced course explores in depth:

- Survey applications
- Thermal imaging
- Photography and video basics

## Flight Exercises

### Precision Maneuvering

Develop exercises that build stick skills systematically. Examples include drawing geometric shapes (who can fly the most precise circle) and positioning exercises over fixed targets.

### Dynamic Reaction Training

An idea worth exploring: a training course with programmable lights that activate randomly, requiring students to navigate to targets under time pressure. Budget approximately $500 for materials to construct training obstacles and targets.

## Topics from Approved Course Outline

The following topics are specified in the official AERO 100 course outline and must be covered.

### Maintenance and Preflight Inspection Procedures

Regulatory requirements for maintenance, preflight and postflight inspection procedures, manufacturer inspection intervals, battery maintenance protocols, flight and maintenance logs, and component maintenance, repair, and replacement.

### UAV Flight Operations

Takeoff, aircraft control, preflight programming, turns around a point, aircraft positioning, altitude control, sensor zoom/pan/tilt, airspeed control, approach, and landing.

### Emergency Procedures

Contingency planning, ground emergencies, inflight emergencies, and emergency communications.

### Human Factors

Health and environmental factors, decision making and judgment, risk management and aeronautical decision making.

### Aircraft Performance

Weight and balance calculations, density altitude, takeoff and climb performance, endurance, landing distance, aerodynamics, and ground effect.

### Aviation Weather

Fronts, stability, clouds, and thunderstorms; fog; structural icing and frost; weather sources for aviation operations including the AWC Home Page (NWS Aviation Weather Center); practical weather sources for remote pilots; and automated weather reporting stations.

### Airport and Off-Airport Operations

Operations at non-tower airports, operations at airports with control towers, off-airport operations, and flying at night.

### Airspace and Navigation

Aeronautical charts, knowing where you can fly, geographical coordinates, chart reading, airspace classifications, correcting for wind drift, and special use airspace.

### Regulations and References

UAS terms and abbreviations, types of UAV and their components and uses, government regulations and procedures publications, FAA chart supplements, advisory circulars, notices to airmen, and aviation media.

## Student Learning Outcomes

From the approved course outline:

1. Demonstrate mastery of FAA Part 107 knowledge topics (airspace, aviation weather, aircraft loading, emergency procedures, crew resource management, aeronautical decision-making, airport operations) by passing a written exam with minimum 80% score.

2. Safely maneuver a small unmanned aerial vehicle through an open test lane using the NIST Level 2 standard course within the prescribed 25-minute time limit.

## Required Text
