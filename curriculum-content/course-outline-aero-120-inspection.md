# AERO 120: Drone Infrastructure Inspection - Topic Outline

This outline sequences the wiki articles that back the inspection course, planned in partnership with PG&E and IBEW 1245. Inspection targets different skills than mapping, precision manual flight, thermal interpretation, and asset knowledge, which is why it stands as its own course. Thermal fundamentals are core content now, with full ITC Level 1 thermography preparation planned as a second phase once the utility partnership is established. Public safety operations have moved to a separate future course. AERO 100 is the prerequisite. Course number and title are placeholders pending Ron's input.

## Unit 1: The Inspection Industry

Why utilities fly drones and what the regional employment pipeline looks like.

- [Infrastructure Inspection Operations](../wiki/topics/infrastructure-inspection-operations.md): zoom and thermal inspection, California utility mandates, reporting, and the asset-specific craft of tower, turbine, bridge, and solar work.
- [PG&E Drone Program and Training Pipeline](../wiki/analysis/pge-drone-program-and-training-pipeline.md): PG&E's pilot qualification ladder, the IBEW 1245 training model, and the articulation precedent this course is built around.

## Unit 2: Thermal Imaging Foundations

The physics a thermographer needs before trusting a single temperature reading.

- [Thermal Imaging](../wiki/topics/thermal-imaging.md): LWIR physics, microbolometers, radiometric data, and the hub for the rest of the thermal unit.
- [Emissivity and Reflected Temperature](../wiki/topics/emissivity-and-reflected-temperature.md): apparent versus true temperature and the infrared mirror trap.
- [Spot Size and Measurement Distance](../wiki/topics/spot-size-and-measurement-distance.md): IFOV, the 3 x 3 pixel rule, and how flying height decides whether a measurement is valid at all.
- [Thermal Tuning and Palettes](../wiki/topics/thermal-tuning-and-palettes.md): level and span, palette selection, and the data-versus-display discipline.

## Unit 3: Interpreting and Reporting Findings

Turning a thermal image into a defensible inspection call.

- [Delta-T Severity Classification](../wiki/topics/delta-t-severity-classification.md): temperature rise over reference, NETA-style severity tiers, and the inspection conditions that make comparisons honest.

## Unit 4: Flying the Job

The flight environment that makes utility inspection its own discipline, and the precision stick skills it demands.

- [Flying Near Energized Infrastructure](../wiki/topics/flying-near-energized-infrastructure.md): compass interference, GPS multipath, minimum approach distances, wire strike, and utility safety culture.
- [Drone Radio Links and Interference](../wiki/topics/drone-radio-links-and-interference.md): the C2 and video links, interference near towers and lines, and lost-link failsafes in exactly the places they get tested.
- [Flight Proficiency and Practice Exercises](../wiki/topics/flight-proficiency-and-practice-exercises.md): the precision manual flight drills, revisited from AERO 100 at the tighter tolerances inspection work demands.
- [Field Operations Best Practices](../wiki/topics/field-operations-best-practices.md): checklists, attention discipline, and go/no-go judgment applied to inspection field days.

## Unit 5: Platforms and Compliance

The equipment and procurement context of utility work.

- [Drone Payloads](../wiki/topics/drone-payloads.md): the dual thermal and high-zoom payload configuration that defines the inspection platform.
- [NDAA Compliance and Blue UAS](../wiki/topics/ndaa-compliance-and-blue-uas.md): federal funding restrictions, the Cleared List, and why platform origin matters to utility and government clients.

## Certification

- [Level 1 Thermography Certification](../wiki/exams/level-1-thermography-certification.md): the industry thermal credential this course aligns with, its providers, cost, and career value, with full exam preparation planned as the course's second phase.
