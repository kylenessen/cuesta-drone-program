# Phlebotomy: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Phlebotomy (Certificate of Specialization)

Total units: 3

Prepares the student for entry level employment as a phlebotomist and eligibility for approved state or national certification exam.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Demonstrate and apply ethical, competent, and safe patient care within the scope of practice for a Phlebotomist.

2. Demonstrate the knowledge/skills necessary to pass the National Certification Exam

3. Demonstrate the knowledge/skills necessary to become employed

**Required Course (3 credits) Units: 3.0**
- PHLB 109A PHLEBOTOMY PHLB 109B PHLEBOTOMY EXTERNSHIP 2.0 1.0
