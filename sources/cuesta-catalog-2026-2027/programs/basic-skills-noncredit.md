# Basic Skills Noncredit: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Ged Preparation (Certificate of Competency)

Total units: noncredit; 72.0-216.0 hours

This program is composed of GED preparation courses. Students who successfully complete the courses will be eligible for the certificate. Upon completion of this certificate, students will have the skills to achieve the GED high school equivalency.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Students will be able to successfully pass the four subject tests for the California State GED exam.

**Required Courses (0 credits) Hours: 72.0-216.0**
- BSNC 501 GED PREPARATION - MATH/SCIENCE 36.0-108.0
- BSNC 502 GED PREPARATION - LANGUAGE ARTS/SOCIAL STUDIES 36.0-108.0
