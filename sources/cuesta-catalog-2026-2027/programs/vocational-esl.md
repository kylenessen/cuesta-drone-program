# Vocational ESL: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Noncredit Vocational English As A Second Language (Certificate of Completion)

Total units: noncredit; 72.0-108.0 hours

This program is composed of two ESL vocational and computer literacy courses. Students who successfully complete both courses will be eligible for the certificate. Upon completion of this certificate, English speakers of other languages will be able to communicate effectively and apply relevant computer skills in workplace environments.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Demonstrate the ability to communicate competently in a variety of workplace environments.

2. Demonstrate the ability to use computers and computer-based technology in the workplace.

**Required Courses (0.0 credits) Hours: 72.0-108.0**
- 36.0-54.0
- VESL 711 VESL 712 WORK-RELATED COMMUNICATION AND COMPUTER LITERACY, LEVEL 1 36.0-54.0
- WORK-RELATED COMMUNICATION AND COMPUTER LITERACY, LEVEL 2
