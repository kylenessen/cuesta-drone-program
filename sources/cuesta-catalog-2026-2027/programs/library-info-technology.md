# Library/Info Technology: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Library Services To Children (Certificate of Specialization)

Total units: 12.0

The Library/Information Technology Program trains students to assist in the management of information in libraries and other venues using print media as well as technology. The program is designed to prepare individuals for employment as a paraprofessional in a public, school, academic, or special library or an information center.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Define key terminology in library information technology and web development technologies.

2. Describe typical workplace duties for the position of library/information technician.

3. Communicate diversity awareness through bulletin board and book displays.

4. Develop a mission statement based on school criteria.

5. Create a book selection policy based on accepted selection standards.

6. Identify two awards commonly given for children’s books.

7. Evaluate important current issues such as censorship, gender, multiculturalism and intellectual freedom as they pertain to young adults.

8. Explain why picture books are an important learning tool for children.

9. Explain why non-fiction and narrative non-fiction are important to young learners.

10. State how the roles of school library personnel impact the achievement scores of students.

**Required Courses (12 credits)**

Units: 12.0

All courses required for the degree must be completed with a grade of $" \mathbb { C } "$ or better.

- LIBT 201 INTRODUCTION TO LIBRARY SERVICES 3.0
- LIBT 210 SCHOOL LIBRARY/MEDIA CENTER SERVICES 3.0
- LIBT 218 CONNECTING ADOLESCENTS WITH LITERATURE AND LIBRARIES 3.0
- CDFS 234 CHILDREN&#x27;S BOOKS AND NARRATIVES 3.0

## Searching And Researching Strategies (Certificate of Specialization)

Total units: 2

This certificate specializes in information literacy by providing training in searching and researching. It focuses specifically on strategies, and concepts to locate a variety of information resources relevant to employment, personal interest, and academic needs. This certificate will prepare students in formulating effective search queries, locating and selecting appropriate sources, as well as retrieving, evaluating, and synthesizing information from these resources.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Identify different search techniques and apply appropriate search queries to locate desired information.

2. Locate, use, and evaluate library, Internet, and other information resources relevant to employment, personal interest, or academic need.

3. Synthesize information and evaluate whether research need has been successfully satisfied.

**Required Courses (2 credits) Units: 2.0**
- LIBT 212 RESEARCH SKILLS FOR INFORMATION AGE 1.0
- LIBT 213 ADVANCED ONLINE SEARCHING 1.0
