## Customer Service (Certificate of Specialization)

Total units: 5.0

**Customer Service Certificate of Specialization**

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Develop basic skills and learn content knowledge in accounting, economics, marketing, management, law, entrepreneurship, international trade, e-commerce, computer applications, and customer service.

2. Achieve a Business Administration A.A. degree that is transferable to a four-year college or university and which can be obtained at Cuesta College within two years.

3. Be trained for immediate entry into, or advancement within, the workforce in business related fields of employment.
