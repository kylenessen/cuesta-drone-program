# Construction Technology: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Construction Technology (Associate in Science)

Total units: 18.5

This program is designed for individuals seeking entry level positions in the construction industry. By fulfilling the program requirements, students will have the necessary knowledge and skills for a career in the construction industry. The students will learn a variety of skills in residential construction such as rough and finish carpentry, plumbing, electrical, concrete, blueprint reading, and construction management.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Demonstrate the skills and knowledge required for an entry level position in the construction industry.

2. Work in a safe manner.

**Required Courses (10.5 credits) Units: 10.5**
- ATCH 105 PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT FOR EMPLOYMENT I 2.0
- CTCH 260 CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT 3.0
- CTCH 200 WORKPLACE SAFETY- OSHA10 0.5
- CTCH 210 BLUEPRINT READING 2.0
- CTCH 250 MATERIALS OF CONSTRUCTION 3.0

- BUS 283 INTRODUCTION TO MANAGEMENT 3.0
- CTCH 180A BUILDING CODE I, ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN PROVISIONS 3.0
- CTCH 220 FOUNDATIONS AND SLABS 1.0
- CTCH 222 FLOOR FRAMING 1.0
- CTCH 224 WALL FRAMING 1.0
- CTCH 226 ROOF CONSTRUCTION 1.0
- CTCH 228 INTRODUCTION TO PLUMBING 1.0
- CTCH 230 INTRODUCTION TO RESIDENTIAL WIRING 1.0
- CTCH 232 FINISH CARPENTRY 1.0
- EET 181 NATIONAL ELECTRICAL CODE 3.0
- EET 183 COMMERCIAL AND INDUSTRIAL WIRING 4.0
- ENGR 201 PLANE SURVEYING 3.0
- ENGR 226 ENGINEERING DRAWING 3.0
- WELD 270A BASIC WELDING 3.0
