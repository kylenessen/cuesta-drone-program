Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Blacksmithing (Certificate of Completion)

Total units: noncredit; 36.0-216.0 hours

This is a Career Development and College Preparation (CDCP) Certificate of Completion for noncredit Blacksmithing.

Learning Outcome(s):

1. Demonstrate safe operation of the forge, anvil, hammer, and related blacksmithing tools.

2. Apply fundamental and mid-level blacksmithing techniques to create functional and decorative metal projects.

3. Identify and select appropriate materials for specific blacksmithing applications.

4. Explore career, trade, and entrepreneurial opportunities related to blacksmithing and metalwork.

**Required Courses (0.0 credits) Hours: 36.0-216.0**
- BLACKSMITHING LEVEL 1A
- VOCE 567A 18.0-108.0
- VOCE 567B BLACKSMITHING LEVEL 1B 18.0-108.0

## Essential Ranching Skills (Certificate of Completion)

Total units: 72.0-432.0

The Essential Ranching Skills program offers workforce preparation skills to students seeking employability in ranching related industries.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Identify the components of and create a business plan for a viable ranching, livestock facility, equine facility, or targeted grazing business.

2. Describe various livestock species anatomy and physiology using correct terminology.

3. Examine and analyze land use planning regulations as they relate to agricultural operations.

4. Analyze and explain how to care for livestock while demonstrating sustainable environmental conditions.

5. Analyze and demonstrate basic construction and maintenance of various livestock animal facilities while caring for livestock.

6. Demonstrate knowledge of general safety procedures and sustainable environmental practices of various livestock facilities.

Students must complete 4 of the 5 available courses to receive a certificate. (0.0 Hours: 72.0-432.0 credits)

- VOCE 565A INTRODUCTION TO FARM MAINTENANCE 18.0-108.0
- VOCE 565B INTRODUCTION TO LIVESTOCK IDENTIFICATION AND HANDLING 18.0-108.0
- VOCE 565C INTRODUCTION TO LAND USE PLANNING AND GRAZING 18.0-108.0
- VOCE 565D INTRODUCTION TO LIVESTOCK HEALTH 18.0-108.0
- VOCE 565E INTRODUCTION TO RANCH/TARGETED GRAZING BUSINESS 18.0-108.0


## Noncredit Basic Internet Skills (Certificate of Completion)

Total units: noncredit; 27.0-72.0 hours

Prepares learners in today's computerized environment to conduct basic academic and professional research, and develop basic online research presentations. Earners will be able to submit applications and tests online, communicate with instructors, registrars, and potential employers via email. These courses will also enable students to participate in job specific training that is increasingly delivered in online formats.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Demonstrate a basic understanding of how to use email.

2. Explain how to manipulate photos to be uploaded to the internet.

3. Demonstrate the ability to use a variety of internet search methods to find information on a variety of topics.

**Required Courses (0.0 credits) Hours: 27.0-72.0**
- VOCE 594 SEARCHING THE WEB 9.0
- VOCE 595 E-MAIL FOR BEGINNERS 9.0-54.0
- VOCE 596 OPTIMIZING PHOTOS FOR THE INTERNET AND EMAIL 9.0
