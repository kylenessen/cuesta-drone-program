# English Second Language: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Esl Citizenship (Certificate of Competency)

Total units: noncredit; 108.0 hours

This is a noncredit certificate that consists of completing two ESL Citizenship courses: ESL 707A: Citizenship Level 1 and ESL 707B: Citizenship Level 2. Upon completion of this certificate, students will be able to communicate personal information effectively, identify major facts in US history and government, and participate in the US citizenship test.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Communicate personal information effectively orally and in writing.

2. Identify major facts in U.S. history and recall the structure and functions of the U.S. federal, state, and local governments.

3. Memorize basic facts and values related to US citizen rights and responsibilities and principles of democracy.

**Required Courses (0.0 credits) Hours: 108.0**
- ESL 707A U. S. CITIZENSHIP, LEVEL 1 54.0
- ESL 707B U.S. CITIZENSHIP, LEVEL 2 54.0


## Noncredit English As A Second Language - Level I (Certificate of Competency)

Total units: noncredit; 162.0-216.0 hours

This program is composed of two literacy level ESL courses. Instruction focuses on literacy-low beginning level listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. Upon successful completion of the two required courses, students are eligible for a certificate of competency. Students who earn the certificate will be able to read basic texts, ask and respond to simple questions, and write simple sentences in English.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Ask and respond to simple questions demonstrating understanding of basic vocabulary.

2. Write simple sentences with basic personal information.

3. Read basic texts.

**Required Courses (0.0 credits) Hours: 162.0-216.0**
- 81.0-108.0
- ESL 701 INTRODUCTION TO LITERACY ESL 702 LITERACY 2 81.0-108.0

## Transitions (Certificate of Competency)

Total units: 360.0-504.0

This is a noncredit certificate that consists of completing two advanced level ESL courses: ESL 797: College and Career Foundations and ESL 798: Transition to College and Career. Upon completion of this certificate, students will have developed the English and academic proficiency level necessary to transition successfully to employment and/or further education.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Explain the content of advanced level and non-adapted readings.

2. Write well-developed and organized essays with proficient language use.

3. Demonstrate proficiency in the use of technology to facilitate language development and the completion of college coursework.

4. Identify local educational and career opportunities.

**Required Courses (0.0 credits) Hours: 360.0-504.0**
- ESL 797 COLLEGE AND CAREER FOUNDATIONS 180.0-252.0
- ESL 798 TRANSITION TO COLLEGE AND CAREER 180.0-252.0
