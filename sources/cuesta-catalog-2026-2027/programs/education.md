# Education: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Career Technical Education Teacherpreparation (Associate in Science)

Total units: 24.0

Prepares students for transfer to earn a Bachelor's Degree in Career and Technical Education for teaching at the High School and Community College levels. The program of study includes courses in the student's CTE discipline, as well as introductory teaching pedagogy and CTE classroom observations.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Perform skills specific to the student's CTE discipline.

2. Discuss teaching strategies and approaches appropriate for students of diverse needs, abilities, and backgrounds.

3. Identify specific career goal in education that fits the student's interest, abilities, personality and aspirations.

Required Courses (6 units)

- EDUC 200 INTRODUCTION TO THE TEACHING PROFESSION 3.0
- HSCI 204 STANDARD FIRST AID AND CPR (CARDIOPULMONARY RESUSCITATION) 3.0

**CTE Concentration (18 units) Units: 18.0**


## High School/Middle School Teacher Pathway (Certificate of Achievement)

Total units: 9

The Middle School/High School Teacher Education Certificate of Achievement will enable students to gain practical experience through classroom observations and interaction with middle school/high school students and faculty. This Certificate is designed to be completed alongside an Associate Degree for Transfer in a specific major. Completion of an Associate Degree for Transfer will prepare students for transfer into a baccalaureate program at junior level status and will facilitate the completion of subject matter requirements for a single subject teacher education program.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Describe the legal, ethical, and professional responsibility of a teacher in California public education, including current promising practices in pedagogy and integration of technology.

2. Demonstrate an understanding of diverse learners and the cultural, economic, political, and historical factors and contexts that impact children’s learning throughout the P-12 system.

3. Discuss and analyze the development of children from conception through adolescence in the physical, social, emotional, and cognitive domains based on theoretical frameworks as they relate to field observations in public middle and high school classrooms.

4. Demonstrate the knowledge and skills within a single subject discipline.

**Required Courses (9 units) Units: 9.0**
- CDFS 201 CHILD GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT 3.0
- EDUC 200 INTRODUCTION TO THE TEACHING PROFESSION 3.0
- EDUC 210 ETHNIC STUDIES FOR EDUCATORS 3.0
