# College Success Studies: Degrees and Certificates

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Academic Skills Bridge To College Credit (Certificate of Completion)

Total units: noncredit; 162.0 hours

This is a noncredit certificate that consists of completing two College Success studies courses: CSS 725: College Learning Strategies and CSS 754: Major and Career Exploration. Upon completion of this certificate, students will be better prepared to transition to credit coursework at the college.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Identify campus resources and use critical thinking strategies to integrate these services into plans for personal success.

2. Employ learning strategies for focusing attention when reading, taking notes, and taking tests.

3. Utilize career resources for major and career exploration.

4. Create a student education plan based on individual values, major and career goals.

**Required Courses (0.0 credits) Hours: 162.0**
- CSS 725 COLLEGE LEARNING STRATEGIES 108.0
- CSS 754 MAJOR AND CAREER EXPLORATION 54.0
