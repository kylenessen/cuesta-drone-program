## Aviation Maintenance Technology (Associate in Science)

Total units: 40.0

The AS in Aviation Maintenance Technology prepares students with the skills and technical knowledge they need to perform maintenance on aircraft in the aviation maintenance industry. Includes general, airframe, and powerplant maintenance subjects required by the Federal Aviation Administration as part of the Aviation Maintenance Technology Program.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Exhibit appropriate documentation procedures.

2. Complete a risk assessment for an aircraft.

3. Read and interpret aircraft schematics.

4. Identify and perform appropriate maintenance for a variety of aircraft issues.

5. Evaluate the air-worthiness of an aircraft.

## Aviation Mechanic - Airframe (Certificate of Achievement)

Total units: 25

This program provides graduates with the knowledge, skills, and risk management elements described in the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) Airman Certification Standards for both the “General” and “Airframe” subjects. Upon successful completion of this program, students will meet regulatory requirements to take the FAA General and Airframe written exams, as well as the FAA Airframe oral and practical exams which lead to issuance of the Aviation Mechanic Certificate with an Airframe Rating.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Demonstrates ability to design and perform a repair of aircraft metallic and non-metallic structures

2. Inspect an aircraft airframe to determine airworthiness in accordance with 14 CFR part 43.

3. Assess and mitigate risks associated with servicing high pressure fluids and gases used in hydraulic and pneumatic systems.

4. Perform inspection of aircraft instrument system to determine operability.

5. Identify risks associated with fuel system maintenance requiring fuel tank entry and defueling of aircraft.

## Aviation Mechanic - General (Certificate of Achievement)

Total units: 10.0

This program provides graduates with the knowledge, skills, and risk management elements described in the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) Airman Certification Standards for the “General” aviation maintenance subjects. Upon successful completion of this program, students will meet regulatory requirements to take the FAA General written exam, which is a requisite to advanced Airframe and Powerplant ratings.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Read and interpret aviation maintenance regulations, forms and publications.

2. Demonstrate ability to safely perform ground operations of aircraft.

3. Identify the effect of weight changes to balance of aircraft.

4. Assess and mitigate risks associated with aircraft electrical systems

5. Perform repair or alteration of an aircraft system or component(s) using drawings, blueprints or system schematics to ensure conformance with aircraft type design.

## Aviation Mechanic - Powerplant (Certificate of Achievement)

Total units: 25

This program provides graduates with the knowledge, skills, and risk management elements described in the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) Airman Certification Standards for both the “General” and “Powerplant” subjects. Upon successful completion of this program, students will meet regulatory requirements to take the FAA General and Powerplant written exams, as well as the FAA Powerplant oral and practical exams which lead to issuance of the Aviation Mechanic Certificate with a Powerplant Rating.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Remove and install a fuel nozzle in a turbine engine.

2. Perform engine inspection requirement in compliance with 14 CFR Part 43 and 14 CFR Part 91.

3. Troubleshoot an engine oil temperature/pressure instrument system and perform necessary repairs to make system operable.

4. Identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with fire protection system extinguishing agents.

5. Inspect engine Perform inspection of aircraft reciprocating engine and determine airworthiness.
