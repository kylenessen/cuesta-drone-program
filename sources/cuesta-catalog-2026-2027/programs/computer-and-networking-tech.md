Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Associate Degree & Certificate Programs (catalog pages 140-331).

## Computer And Networking Technology (Associate in Science)

Total units: 28.0-31.0

The Computer and Network Technology program builds a solid foundation in computer and networking theory and applications. The training includes copper, fiber optics and wireless network infrastructure, computer and networking hardware, desktop and server operating systems, computer security, cloud computing, and computer and network diagnostic tools. The goal of the degree is to provide broad training that will prepare a student to install, configure, troubleshoot, and administrate all types of computers and network hardware and associated operating systems. Each course in the program is aligned with industry certification objectives including CompTIA A+, Server+, Security+, Cisco Network Academy, and Cloud Computing.

Learning Outcome(s):

1. Use knowledge of fundamental principles of logic, reasoning, and theory applied to common computing technologies and products.

2. Develop industry applicable skills including analytical and critical thinking, technical communication and project management that will enable the technician to adapt to many jobs and changing requirements of industry.

3. Apply knowledge and skills to design and assemble platforms, operate and maintain, analyze, troubleshoot and repair relevant computing applications, technologies and systems needed to support local industries.

4. Demonstrate traits of hard work, self-motivation, personal integrity, personal responsibility, teamwork and collaboration that will contribute to their success as well as the success of their employers.

## Computer Networking Support (Certificate of Specialization)

Total units: 9.0

The Computer Networking Support Certificate prepares students for entry level Network Technician and Cyber Security certifications. Networking and Cyber Security certifications enable students to begin or advance their career in the IT industry, specializing in network maintenance, cyber security operations, and administration.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Create secure networks and computer systems using appropriate vulnerability and risk management techniques.

2. Design a secure physical and logical architecture of a typical medium size business network: cabling and device interconnection, switch selection, and Internet access, and IP addressing schemes.

3. Use distance vector routing protocols and network discovery tools to determine route summarization, perform routing table maintenance, and detect routing loops for RIPv2 and OSPF.

4. Perform Inter-VLAN Routing configuration and troubleshooting. Configure interfaces and subinterfaces using Router-on-a-Stick Configuration. Describe and resolve the associated IP addressing issues.

5. Troubleshoot typical business networks: describe methodologies and use a variety of software and hardware tools to analyze network performance.



**Required Courses (9 units) Units: 9.0**
- CNET 260 NETWORKING FUNDAMENTALS 2.5
- CNET 261 ROUTER THEORY AND TECHNOLOGY 2.5
- CNET 235 CYBER SECURITY FUNDAMENTALS 4.0


## Computer Support Specialist (Certificate of Specialization)

Total units: 13.0

The Computer Support Specialist Certificate prepares students for entry level positions in computer repair and operating system administration for both physical and cloud computing applications. The courses are aligned with, and help prepare for, the industry standard CompTIA A+, Server+, and Cloud Computing certifications.

**Learning Outcome(s):**

1. Demonstrate the skills and knowledge to repair and configure computer hardware and software.

2. Demonstrate proficiency in configuring and administering networked operating systems for both physical and cloud computing applications.
