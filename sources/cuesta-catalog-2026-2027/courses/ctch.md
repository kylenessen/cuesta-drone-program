Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## CTCH 180A BUILDING CODE I, ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN PROVISIONS (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Advisories: CTCH 250

Introduction to the Building Code, concentrating on the permit process and fundamental architectural design requirements involving occupancies, maximum allowable area, type of construction, proximity to property lines, exiting, fire protection, disabled access, elevators, and construction safety. This course is the first in a series of two which will familiarize the student with building code provisions relating to the design and construction of residential and commercial structures. The emphasis will be on requirements which limit or specify the building space to be created.

## CTCH 200 WORKPLACE SAFETY- OSHA10 (0.50 units)

- Hours: 0.7 hours per week: (0.67 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Provides basic safety and health information and education for the construction industry that fulfills the OSHA10 requirements. Provides training for workers and employers in safety and the recognition, avoidance, abatement, and prevention of health hazards in workplaces in the construction industry. Also provides information regarding workers' rights, employer responsibilities, and how to file a complaint.
