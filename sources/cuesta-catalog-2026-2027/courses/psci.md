# Physical Science (PSCI)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## PSCI 210 ENERGY AND MATTER (4.00 units)

- Hours: 6 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/3.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: Intermediate algebra or eligibility for college-level math using the current college process.

Investigates basic principles of physics and chemistry. This activity-based course provides an introduction to the basic concepts of physical science with emphasis on their practical importance and application in the real world. Designed for nonscience majors, concepts are introduced in lab through inquiry and further developed during discussion.

Transfer: CSU; UC C-ID:CHEM 140

## PSCI 211 EARTH SCIENCE (4.00 units)

- Hours: 6 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/3.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: Elementary algebra or eligibility for college-level math using the current college process.

Introduces the essentials of Earth Science including a laboratory component. Topics include the geosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, and solar system. This course focuses on the interactions between physical and chemical systems of the Earth such as the tectonic cycle, rock cycle, hydrologic cycle, weather and climate.

Transfer: CSU; UC C-ID:GEOL 121

## PSCI 220 ENERGY, ELECTRICITY, AND THE ENVIRONMENT (0.50 units)

- Hours: 1 hours per week: (0.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/ 1.00 activity hours/0.00 work experience hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Not Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Introduces students to basic theoretical and practical concepts in electrical energy production, measurements, usage, and storage. Provides opportunities to research and analyze potential environmental impacts related to the methods in which energy is produced.

Transfer: CSU
