# Aviation Maintenance (AVMT)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## AVMT 110 AVIATION GENERAL MAINTENANCE I (5.00 units)

- Hours: 12 hours per week: (2.00 lecture hours/10.00 lab hours/0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Prepares students with the skills and technical knowledge they need to perform maintenance on aircraft in the aviation maintenance industry. Includes general aviation maintenance subjects required by the Federal Aviation Administration as part of the Aviation Maintenance Technology Program. Covers fundamental electricity and electronics; aircraft weight and balance; mathematics; physics for aviation; ground operations and servicing; and human factors IMPORTANT: Students are required to provide certain hand-tools at their own expense, to successfully complete the course requirements.

## AVMT 120 AVIATION GENERAL MAINTENANCE II (5.00 units)

- Hours: 12 hours per week: (2.00 lecture hours/10.00 lab hours/0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Prerequisites: AVMT 110 concurrently or AVMT 110 or AVMT 110A previously and AVMT 110B concurrently OR AVMT 110B previously and AVMT 110A concurrently

Prepares students with the skills and technical knowledge they need to perform maintenance on aircraft in the aviation maintenance industry. Completes general aviation maintenance subjects required by the Federal Aviation Administration for issuance of a general aviation maintenance technician certificate. Covers maintenance forms, records, and publications; mechanic privileges and limitations; aircraft material, hardware, and processes; aircraft drawings; fluid lines and fittings; cleaning and corrosion control; inspection concepts and techniques. IMPORTANT: Students are required to provide certain hand-tools at their own expense, to successfully complete the course requirements.
