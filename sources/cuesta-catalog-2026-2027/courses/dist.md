# Distance (DIST)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## DIST 101 INTRODUCTION TO ONLINE COURSES (0.50 units)

- Hours: 1.5 hours per week: (0.00 lecture hours/1.50 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Introduces students to the types of technologies utilized in a distance education course and the academic and technological skills needed to be successful in distance education courses.
