# Licensed Vocational Nursing (LVN)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## LVN 101 THEORY I: FUNDAMENTALS, PERIOPERATIVE AND ACUTE ADULT (7.00 units)

- Hours: 7 hours per week: (7.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Prerequisites: Program admission
- Corequisites: LVN 101A and LVN 101AL and LVN 101B

Prepares students to practice vocational nursing. Uses the Caring Framework for the study of nursing fundamentals, nursing process, and standard nursing care for a variety of common health and illness states. Compares and contrasts nursing care systems and roles employed in health maintenance, acute illness, surgical care, rehabilitation nursing for adults and elderly, and perioperative nursing. Prepares the vocational student nurse to care for patients and integrate concepts of perioperative nursing, adapting standard nursing care to meet the needs of the elderly is a focus for the LVN role.

Provides an opportunity for supervised practice of basic nursing skills in a simulated clinical setting. ## LVN 104 BASIC INTRAVENOUS THERAPY, BLOOD ADMINISTRATION, AND PHLEBOTOMY (1.50 units)

- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: LVN 103B or May have current and active LVN license from the Board of Vocational Nursing and Psychiatric Technicians of the State of California.
- Corequisites: LVN 104L

Prepares the Licensed Vocational Nurse who has successfully completed LVN 103B or its equivalent to initiate intravenous therapy, phlebotomy, and blood product transfusion. This course prepares the LVN for certification that meets standards of the California State Board of Vocational Nurses and Psychiatric Technicians.
