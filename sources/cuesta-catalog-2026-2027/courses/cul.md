# Culinary Arts (CUL)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## CUL 110 SURVEY OF BASIC CULINARY SKILLS (1.00 units)

- Hours: 2 hours per week: (0.50 lecture hours/1.50 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Designed for students interested in an overview of basic culinary skills, food preparation, nutrition, cleaning, and safety methods. Students will gain hands-on training of skills, techniques and methods needed to follow and prepare recipes, conduct proper food handling, safety and cleaning methods, along with the skills and techniques needed to prepare quality food.

## CUL 210 CULINARY ARTS FUNDAMENTALS I (3.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (2.00 lecture hours/3.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: CUL 215 or CUL 213 or equivalent food handlers certificate or concurrent enrollment in any one of the above

Introduces the student to the culinary arts profession and the professional kitchen. Emphasis on basic cooking methods, equipment use, and ingredients provides the student with a beginning understanding of food chemistry and cooking techniques. The course includes "hands-on" experience managing the kitchen in the production of stocks, broths, sauces, gravies, soups, and breakfast cookery.

## CUL 780 OUTDOOR COOKING (0.00 units)

- Hours: 1.7 hours per week: (0.56 lecture hours/1.12 lab hours)
- Credit status: Non-credit Course

Non Credit Prepares students for outdoor site cooking with a focus on the methods of safe food transportation, basic meal planning, and outdoor preparation of whole foods and balanced meals during CCC (California Conservation Corps) team excursions. Applicable tasks will include preliminary food preparation, planning, and packing meals. Applicable skills will include building and maintaining a fire for cooking, utilizing four main cooking methods, and application of the fundamental concepts of whole foods cooking to an outdoor kitchen environment.

## CUL 790 ADVANCED OUTDOOR COOKING (0.00 units)

- Hours: 1.3 hours per week: (0.67 lecture hours/0.67 lab hours)
- Credit status: Non-credit Course

Non Credit Provides students with preparation techniques and readiness for outdoor site cooking through advanced meal planning and a deeper study of whole food nutrition. Provides experience in preparation of shelf-stable foods onsite and backcountry cooking techniques often associated with cooking team meals for CCC (California Conservation Corps) excursions. Applicable topics and scope of learning will include food preservation techniques such as dehydration, fermentation, and vacuum sealing, meal planning, and packing meals for remote areas. Applicable skills will include Indigenous cooking methods and back country 'leave no trace' practices.
