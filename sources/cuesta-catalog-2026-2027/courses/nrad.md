Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## NRAD 102H BRIDGE PATHWAY TO RN READINESS (1.00 units)

- Hours: 1.5 hours per week: (0.75 lecture hours/0.75 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: P/NP Only
- Prerequisites: Pre-course testing, TEAS RN, Selection based on LVN to RN requirements Corequisites: NRAD 202B

Designed to prepare the licensed vocational nurse (LVN) for entry into the 2nd semester of an associate degree nursing (ADN) program. Emphasizes planning, implementation, and evaluation of the nursing process. Assessment of nursing competencies expected of the graduate ADN, including critical thinking skills, review of medical-surgical nursing, pharmacology, and medication calculations to prepare the student for success in an ADN program.
