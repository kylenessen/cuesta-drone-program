# Water Sciences and Operations (WATR)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## WATR 270 BASIC WATER TREATMENT (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Prepares the student to take the California's State Water Treatment Plant Operator, Grade 1 or Grade 2 exam. Includes the study of plant operation, water sources and treatment, reservoir management, coagulation and flocculation, sedimentation, filtration, disinfection, taste and odor control, and corrosion control.

Transfer: CSU

## WATR 272 WATER DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Prepares the student to take the California's State Water Distribution Plant Operator, Grade 1 exam. Provides an understanding of the basic operational and maintenance concepts of water distribution systems. Develops the ability to analyze and solve problems such as tastes and odors, corrosion damage, and system failures when they occur.

Transfer: CSU
