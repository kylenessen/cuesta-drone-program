# French (FR)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## FR 201 FRENCH I (5.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Presents a topic-based introduction to contemporary French language and culture with an emphasis on listening comprehension, oral interaction, culture-centered reading and writing basic French. Social and cultural aspects of the French-speaking world are presented along with some of the most essential structures and vocabulary.

Transfer: CSU; UC

## FR 202 FRENCH II (5.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: FR 201

Presents a continuation of FR 201 with intensive, topic-based practice in oral interaction, culturecentered reading and writing, and listening comprehension using recordings and video clips from the French-speaking world.

Transfer: CSU; UC

## FR 247 INDEPENDENT STUDIES: FRENCH (0.50 to 2.00 units)

- Hours: 1.5 to 6 hours per week: (0.00 - 0.00 lecture hours/ 1.50 - 6.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: The student must have completed preparatory course work in the field in which the study is to be undertaken.

Provides an opportunity for in-depth study by an individual student in a selected topic of French under the direction of an instructor.

Transfer: CSU
