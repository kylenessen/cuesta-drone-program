## PHYS 208AX DIRECTED PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS FOR PHYS 208A (1.00 units)

- Hours: 1 hours per week: (1.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: P/NP Only
- Corequisites: PHYS 208A

Presents problem-solving and fundamenta reasoning skills needed for success in PHYS 208A, Principles of Physics I. This course is designed for students who are currently enrolled in PHYS 208A and seek to improve their problem solving skills and mastery of concepts presented in PHYS 208A. Topics include specific techniques for solving kinematics, force, energy, momentum, torque, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and simple harmonic motion problems, analysis of word problems, and the development of solution maps.

Transfer: CSU
