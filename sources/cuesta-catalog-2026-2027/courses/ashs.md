## ASHS 205 LIFE MANAGEMENT (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Articulates skills to understand and utilize internal and external resources to function effectively in contemporary US society. Major topics include socio-cultural forces that affect personal values, beliefs, behaviors and goal setting; decision-making strategies, time management and stress-reduction strategies; and conflict resolution methods. Strategies and skills for improving self-esteem, selfcare, self-understanding, and interpersonal relationships are also introduced.

Transfer: CSU

## ASHS 210 INTRODUCTION TO SOCIAL WORK AND HUMAN SERVICES (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Examines an introductory overview of U.S. social welfare, social work, and social institutions that deliver the provision of human support services. Presents a historical perspective on the development of the U.S. social work and human services profession with emphasis on delivery systems and protocols. Particular attention is given to policies and procedures that uphold antiracism and social equity practices that support individual and family holistic functioning.

Transfer: CSU; UC C-ID:SWHS 110

## ASHS 218 HUMAN SEXUALITY: EXPERIENCE & EXPRESSION (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Studies the complex psychological, sociological and physiological aspects of human sexuality. Introduces theoretical frameworks that explain sexual development and identity; sexual behaviors; gendered patterns and gender roles; and sexual satisfaction. Examines the societal constructions that define ab/normal sexual expression and the changing definitions of subjective experiences. Discusses sexual response processes, dysfunctions, reproductive health, and sexual violence and healing pathways.

## ASHS 262 CHICANX AND LATINX COMMUNITY PRACTICE (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Advisories: ETHN 230 or ETHN 260

Examines historical and contemporary efforts to address patterns of inequities affecting Chicanx and Latinx communities. Introduces key concepts for understanding equity, policy change, and community wellbeing among Chicanx and Latinx communities. Covers intergenerational trauma, medical exploitation, eugenics, intersectional disparities, barriers to accessing care, Latino Threat Narrative, cultural humility, advocacy, ethnic enclaves, and culturally responsive interventions. Pays special attention to the consequences of inequities in shaping community wellbeing. Same as ETHN 262.

Transfer: CSU; UC

## ASHS 270 INTRO TO ADDICTION STUDIES:ADDICTION AND THE COMMUNITY (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Explores historical, psychological, and sociological perspectives on the use, abuse, and social control of psychoactive drugs. Presents in depth analysis of the theoretical models of the biopsychosocial nature of addiction, the dynamics of addiction, the impact of addiction on children, families and society with emphasis on special populations, contemporary treatment and prevention approaches, and the addiction counseling profession.

Transfer: CSU
