## CJ 202 INTRODUCTION TO CRIMINAL JUSTICE (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Presents an overview of the history, theory, organization and practices of the criminal justice system and the interrelationship between the criminal justice system and society as a whole. Studies the origin, development and application of crime causation theories. Includes exploration and analysis of the roles, structure, and function of criminal justice agencies. Integrates an examination of the criminal justice system with a critical analysis of the system as an instrument of societal control and change.

Transfer: CSU; UC C-ID:AJ 110

## CJ 206 CONCEPTS OF CRIMINAL LAW (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Presents elements of the penal code and general laws about crimes against persons, property, standards of morality, and the state. Includes the procedures to be followed in evidence collection and preservation.

## CJ 224 COMMUNITY, DIVERSITY AND THE JUSTICE SYSTEM (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Explores the complex dynamic relationships between the community and the justice system. Examines the role that race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, social class and culture play in the administration of justice. ## CJ 260 INTRODUCTION TO CYBERCRIME (4.00 units)

- Hours: 4 hours per week: (4.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Provides students with an introduction to the fundamental knowledge needed when assessing, investigating, and safeguarding entities from hightech and cybercrimes. Topics include the history of computer crimes, cyber threats, cybercrimes, cybersecurity, including the Internet and communication technologies. The course explores hacking methodology, and the psychology behind the cybercriminal. Cybersecurity essentials are explored, including the hardening of systems and daily cyber operations for multiple OS platforms, including Windows and Linux administration, networking fundamentals, and the basics of cloud computing.

Transfer: CSU

## CJ 262 ETHICAL HACKING (4.00 units)

- Hours: 4 hours per week: (4.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Examines foundational concepts in offensive cybersecurity operations, including penetration testing, risk assessment and management, incident response, and the implementation of security controls. Emphasis is placed on developing handson technical skills through practical exercises. Topics include defense-in-depth strategies, network and system attacks, digital fingerprinting, port scanning, enumeration, digital forensics, cryptography, and the identification and mitigation of operating system vulnerabilities.

Transfer: CSU
