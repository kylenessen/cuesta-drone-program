## KINE 210 ATHLETIC TRAINING AND SPORTS MEDICINE LAB LEVEL 1 (2.00 to 4.00 units)

- Hours: 6 to 12 hours per week: (0.00 - 0.00 lecture hours/ 6.00 - 12.00 lab hours/0.00 - 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Provides the beginning level athletic training student practical, hands-on experience within the field of sports medicine and athletic training. It is designed to present the student with knowledge of injury prevention and care by observing procedure, techniques and concepts applicable to the athletic training setting. Experiences will include observing taping for prevention of injury, use of modalities, and exercise programs in rehabilitation of injuries.

## KINE 221 MENTAL ASPECTS OF SPORTS AND EXERCISE PERFORMANCE (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3 hours per week: (3.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Evaluates scientific approaches to mental aspects of sports and exercise performance and apply practical application of that knowledge. Examines psychological factors that influence sport performance and/or exercise adherence, such as personality, cognitive processes, motivation, social dynamics, and leadership, are present in the course. Contrasts mental skills training methods such as arousal management, imagery, and goal setting is introduced. Analyzes the relationship between sport/exercise participation, and psychological health, wellness, and development is also initiated.

Transfer: CSU; UC

## KINE 253 LIFEGUARD TRAINING (1.00 units)

- Hours: 1.5 hours per week: (1.00 lecture hours/0.50 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP

Provides an opportunity to introduce and develop the basic lifesaving skills necessary to successfully serve as a lifeguard. Includes discussion and practice of lifeguard skills, including assessment of an emergency situation, rescue techniques, first aid, CPR/AED, and the use of emergency equipment. Qualifies students who successfully pass written and practical exams to be certified for an American Red Cross Lifeguard Training, First Aid, and CPR for the Professional Rescuer.

Transfer: CSU; UC
