# Medical Assisting (MAST)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## MAST 110 MEDICAL ASSISTING BASICS (2.00 units)

- Hours: 2 hours per week: (2.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Advisories: NRAD 222

Present the role of the medical assistant along with concepts of ethics, law, professionalism, and computer basics to facilitate entry level medical assisting practice.

## MAST 111A MEDICAL ASSISTING FAST TRACK (9.50 units)

- Hours: 11.3 hours per week: (8.78 lecture hours/2.56 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Prerequisites: Student must be 18 years of age by the first class meeting MAST 110 or
- Corequisites: MAST 110 MAST 111B and Current Health Care Provider CPR and Standard First Aid Advisories: NRAD 222

Prepares the student for entry level employment as a medical assistant and eligibility for approved state certification exam. Must be 18 years of age by the first day of class to be certified.

## MAST 111B MEDICAL ASSISTING FAST TRACK LAB (1.00 units)

- Hours: 3.3 hours per week: (0.00 lecture hours/3.34 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: P/NP Only
- Prerequisites: Student must be 18 yrs of age by the first class meeting.
- Corequisites: MAST 111A and Current Health Care Provider CPR and Standard First Aid

Provides the opportunity to apply theory during externship. Includes placement in a community front and back office site offering the opportunity to perform various skills, practice communication techniques, and learn about professionalism under the direction of a preceptor. Provides experience which contributes to marketability for employment as a medical assistant and eligibility for approved state or national certification exam. Must be 18 years or older to be certified.
