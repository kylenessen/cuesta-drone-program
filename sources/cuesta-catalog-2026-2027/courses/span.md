## SPAN 201 SPANISH I (5.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only

Presents a topic-based, introductory language acquisition course in standard contemporary Spanish, with emphasis on listening comprehension, oral interaction, culture-centered readings, and writing of basic Spanish. Social and cultural aspects of the Spanish-speaking world are presented along with the most essential structures and vocabulary. Typed written exercises and online multimedia practice activities are required.

Transfer: CSU; UC C-ID:SPAN 100

## SPAN 202 SPANISH II (5.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Prerequisites: SPAN 201

Presents a continuation of SPAN 201 with a topiccentered, intensive practice in listening comprehension, oral interaction, and writing. Selected readings from the Hispanic world include cultural vignettes, prose selections, and poetry. Typed written exercises and online multimedia practice activities are required.

Transfer: CSU; UC C-ID:SPAN 110

## SPAN 203 SPANISH III (5.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Prerequisites: SPAN 202

Presents a continuation of SPAN 202 incorporating a review with topic-centered intensive practice in listening comprehension, oral interaction, and paragraph writing. Selected readings from the Hispanic world include social, cultural, and historythemed vignettes, prose selections, and poetry. Typed written exercises and online multimedia practice activities are required.

Transfer: CSU; UC C-ID:SPAN 200

## SPAN 204 SPANISH IV (5.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Prerequisites: SPAN 203

Presents an expansion of SPAN 203 focusing on 4 skills: listening , conversation, reading and presentational writing. Drawing from authentic materials, SPAN 204 is an intermediate course that provides a comprehensive grammar review, extensive oral practice and an introduction to literary concepts. Competency reflects the students' emerging ability to perform both orally and in written form with complex situations. Online multimedia practice is required and includes audio and video and extensive online resources.

Transfer: C-ID:SPAN 210; CSU; UC

## SPAN 215 SPANISH FOR SPANISH SPEAKERS I (4.00 units)

- Hours: 4 hours per week: (4.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: SPAN 202

Develops and refines Spanish-language use for students who already have experience with the language. This course is designed for students who already speak Spanish and wish to improve and hone their skills. In addition to basic grammar and vocabulary development, this course practices writing, spelling, dialectal differences, and distinguishing between formal and informal language use.

Transfer: CSU; UC

## SPAN 230 SPANISH FOR SPANISH SPEAKERS II (4.00 units)

- Hours: 4 hours per week: (4.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/ 0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: SPAN 215 or SPAN 203

Continues to develop and refine Spanish-language use for students who already have experience with the language. This course is designed for students who already speak Spanish and wish to improve and hone their skills. In addition to more advanced grammar and vocabulary development, this course practices writing, spelling, dialectal differences, and distinguishing between formal and informal language use at a higher level.

Transfer: CSU; UC
