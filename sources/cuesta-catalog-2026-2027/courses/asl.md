# American Sign Language (ASL)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## ASL 147 INDEPENDENT STUDIES: AMERICANSIGN LANGUAGE (0.50 to 2.00 units)

- Hours: 1.5 to 6 hours per week: (0.00 - 0.00 lecture hours/ 1.50 - 6.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: The student must have completed preparatory course work in the field in which the study is to be undertaken.

Provides an opportunity for an in-depth study or practicum experience by an individual student in a selected topic of American Sign Language under the direction of an instructor. ASL 201 AMERICAN SIGN LANGUAGE I 5.00 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours) Credit - Degree Applicable Introduces a functional-notional approach to the acquisition of American Sign Language (ASL) - a visual/gestural language - through the development of visual receptive and expressive skills required for vocabulary development, grammar, non-manual behaviors, and fingerspelling. Creates awareness of Deaf Culture by reviewing the history of ASL, dynamics of the Deaf community and cultural customs of communication.

Transfer: CSU; UC

## ASL 202 AMERICAN SIGN LANGUAGE II (5.00 units)

- Hours: 5 hours per week: (5.00 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade or P/NP
- Prerequisites: ASL 201

Presents a continuation of ASL 201 through vocabulary development, grammar, non-manual behaviors and fingerspelling incorporating Deaf culture throughout. Heightens sensitivity to Deaf culture, including its history, values, traditions and communication customs.

Transfer: CSU; UC
