# Nursing Assistant (NAST)

Source: Cuesta College 2026-2027 Catalog (July 2026 Addendum), Courses of Instruction.

## NAST 148 NURSING ASSISTANT (3.00 units)

- Hours: 3.3 hours per week: (3.34 lecture hours/0.00 lab hours/0.00 activity hours)
- Credit status: Credit - Degree Applicable
- Grading: Letter Grade Only
- Corequisites: NAST 148L

Presents theory necessary for basic nursing care. Upon completion of this course, students are eligible to take the National Nurse Assistant Training and Assessment Program Test (NNAAP). Based on state background clearance and success on the NNAAP, students will obtain a certificate as a Certified Nurse Assistant in the state of California.
