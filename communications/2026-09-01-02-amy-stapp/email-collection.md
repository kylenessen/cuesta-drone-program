**Program research.** Amy points to Hancock’s recent submission, its Criminal Investigation elective, and the SLO labor market information. These raise questions about how Cuesta’s program should be positioned and whether Criminal Justice should be involved. See message 1.

**Industry contacts.** Luke Longman is already introduced and copied at lukemlongman@gmail.com. CES Utility Solutions is the company Amy identifies. Introductions at Inspired Flight and Saga Robotics are offers awaiting a response in the collected messages. See messages 2 through 5.

**Agricultural application training.** Edwin’s forwarded note points to CeRae Spaidel at 805-781-5910 for county pesticide program guidance. His licensing and curriculum comments are preserved as email content, without independent verification. See message 6.

**Funding.** The Agriculture Expansion project was approved for $125,488 in Round 11. The email also mentions remaining Round 10 Agriculture funds. It says allocations may change, reporting is required, and the funding is not a direct departmental budget allocation. The share available specifically for drone work is not stated. Jennifer Madrid is the contact for balance questions and Janine Medina for getting started. See message 7.
