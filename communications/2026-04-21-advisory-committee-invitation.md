## Summary

This email formally invited Sam, Sully, and Madison to serve on the Industry Advisory Committee for Cuesta College's new UAS program, which Ron and I are developing. All three had already expressed interest in lending their expertise. The invitation attached the working proposal of the planned courses and asked each person to review the sections relevant to their work.

The timing was driven by a meeting with our Dean that week. I asked for replies by end of day Thursday, April 23, so we could show the Dean that the program is being built in active dialogue with industry advisors. I made clear that a quick reaction was enough at that stage. Full feedback was welcome but not required.

## Questions posed to the committee

1. Does what we described sound useful for the work you do?
2. Is there anything important we are missing? As an example, I noted that all three advisors had flagged the industry shift away from DJI, and the proposal did not address that at all.
3. Does anything seem off or misaligned with where the industry is headed?

## Committee recruitment

The email also noted that the committee has room for additional members. I asked each recipient to pass along names from their networks, or to forward the invitation directly.

## Context

The advisor roster spans the program's three target markets. Sully works in environmental consulting at Althouse and Meade, Sam works in public safety at the California Office of Emergency Services, and Madison flies for PG&E's utility inspection program. The DJI question raised in this email is now addressed in the course outlines through the NDAA compliance content and the equipment sections that invite committee guidance on adding a compliant aircraft.
